Section 01

Why Convert Analog to Digital Data?

Digital signals are vastly superior to analog signals because they are robust to noise, easily regenerated by repeaters without noise accumulation, and compatible with digital error detection/correction algorithms.

Technique 1
🎙️ Pulse Code Modulation (PCM)
The standard digitization method using 3 steps: Sampling $\rightarrow$ Quantization $\rightarrow$ Binary Encoding.
Technique 2
📉 Delta Modulation (DM)
A simplified 1-bit technique that records only the relative difference (+1 or -1) between consecutive samples.
📈
Section 02

PCM Step 1: Sampling & Nyquist Theorem

Sampling measures the continuous analog signal amplitude at fixed time intervals $T_s = 1/f_s$.

Method A
1. Ideal Sampling
An impulse train samples the signal at exact instantaneous points.
Method B
2. Natural Sampling
Short width pulses whose tops retain the natural shape of the analog wave.
Method C
3. Flat-Top Sampling (PAM)
Sample-and-hold circuit creates flat-topped pulses (Pulse Amplitude Modulation).
⚡ Nyquist Sampling Theorem
$$f_s \ge 2 \times f_{\max}$$
The sampling frequency $f_s$ must be at least twice the highest frequency component $f_{\max}$ to prevent aliasing distortion!
📊
Section 03

PCM Step 2: Quantization & Step Size $\Delta$

🔥 IBPS SO IT – Top Numerical Area

Quantization maps continuous sample amplitude values into $L$ discrete quantization levels.

⚡ Quantization Step Size Formula
$$\Delta = \frac{V_{\max} - V_{\min}}{L}$$
$V_{\max}, V_{\min}$ = voltage boundaries  |  $L = 2^n$ = number of levels
📝
Worked Example (Forouzan Slide 4.8)
- Voltage range: $V_{\min} = -20\text{V}$, $V_{\max} = +20\text{V}$. Number of levels $L = 8$.
- Step size $\Delta = \frac{+20 - (-20)}{8} = \frac{40}{8} = \mathbf{5\text{V}}$.
- 8 Level Midpoints: $-17.5\text{V}, -12.5\text{V}, -7.5\text{V}, -2.5\text{V}, +2.5\text{V}, +7.5\text{V}, +12.5\text{V}, +17.5\text{V}$.
- Maximum Quantization Error = $\frac{\Delta}{2} = \frac{5}{2} = \mathbf{\pm 2.5\text{V}}$.
🔣
Section 04

PCM Step 3: Binary Encoding & SQNR Rule

⚡ Bits per Sample & Bit Rate
$$n = \log_2(L)$$
$$\text{PCM Bit Rate} = f_s \times n = (2 \times f_{\max}) \times n$$
$n$ = number of bits per sample
⚡ Signal-to-Quantization Noise Ratio (SQNR) Rule
$$\text{SQNR}_{\text{dB}} = 6.02 n + 1.76 \text{ dB} \approx 6n \text{ dB}$$
Rule of Thumb: Each additional bit used in quantization improves SQNR by approximately 6 dB!
📉
Section 05

Delta Modulation (DM) & Impairments

Delta Modulation transmits only **1 bit per sample** indicating whether the current sample is higher (`1`) or lower (`0`) than the previous staircase estimate.

DM Impairment 1
Slope Overload Distortion
Occurs when the analog signal changes rapidly, but the fixed step size $\delta$ is too small to keep up with the steep slope.
DM Impairment 2
Granular Noise
Occurs when the analog signal is relatively flat, but the fixed step size $\delta$ causes the staircase to oscillate above and below the signal.
🔌
Section 06

Transmission Modes (Parallel vs. Serial)

Mode Wiring / Clocking Features & Bit Framing Primary Application
Parallel Transmission $n$ separate wires for $n$ bits simultaneously Very fast, expensive cables. Cross-talk over long distances. Computer internal bus, legacy printer ports.
Serial Asynchronous 1 wire, no shared clock line Bits sent in bytes framed with Start bit (0) and Stop bit (1). Gaps allowed between bytes. RS-232, UART, keyboard/mouse.
Serial Synchronous 1 wire, synchronized clocking Continuous bit stream framed in large blocks without start/stop bits. High efficiency. High-speed LANs, WAN links (HDLC, PPP).

Section 08

IBPS SO IT PYQ Practice Questions

PYQ 01 Quantization Step Size
An analog signal with voltage range from $-10\text{V}$ to $+10\text{V}$ is quantized using $L = 16$ levels. What is the quantization step size ($\Delta$)?
  • A. $0.625\text{V}$
  • B. $1.25\text{V}$ ✓
  • C. $2.5\text{V}$
  • D. $5\text{V}$
Answer: B — 1.25 V
$\Delta = \frac{V_{\max} - V_{\min}}{L} = \frac{+10 - (-10)}{16} = \frac{20}{16} = \mathbf{1.25\text{V}}$.
PYQ 02 SQNR Improvement Per Bit
In PCM, adding 1 extra bit to the quantization encoder ($n \rightarrow n+1$) improves the Signal-to-Quantization Noise Ratio (SQNR) by approximately:
  • A. 3 dB
  • B. 6 dB ✓
  • C. 10 dB
  • D. 12 dB
Answer: B — 6 dB
$\text{SQNR}_{\text{dB}} \approx 6.02 n + 1.76\text{ dB}$. Increasing $n$ by 1 increases SQNR by $\approx 6\text{ dB}$.
PYQ 03 Slope Overload Distortion
Slope Overload Distortion in Delta Modulation (DM) occurs when the input analog signal:
  • A. Remains flat and constant
  • B. Changes at a rate faster than the step size can follow ✓
  • C. Has zero frequency
  • D. Exceeds Nyquist sampling rate
Answer: B
Slope overload distortion happens when the analog signal rises or falls very rapidly, but the fixed step size $\delta$ cannot keep up with the steep slope.
PYQ 04 Asynchronous Start Bit
In Asynchronous serial transmission, what is the binary value of the Start Bit used to alert the receiver of incoming data?
  • A. Binary 0 ✓
  • B. Binary 1
  • C. Alternating 0 and 1
  • D. High voltage +V
Answer: A — Binary 0
In asynchronous transmission, the idle line state is 1 (mark). A Start Bit of binary `0` (space) drops the line voltage to signal data arrival.
PYQ 05 PCM Steps Sequence
What is the correct sequence of operations in a Pulse Code Modulation (PCM) encoder?
  • A. Quantization $\rightarrow$ Sampling $\rightarrow$ Encoding
  • B. Sampling $\rightarrow$ Quantization $\rightarrow$ Binary Encoding ✓
  • C. Encoding $\rightarrow$ Quantization $\rightarrow$ Sampling
  • D. Sampling $\rightarrow$ Modulation $\rightarrow$ Quantization
Answer: B — Sampling $\rightarrow$ Quantization $\rightarrow$ Binary Encoding
1. Sampler creates PAM pulses $\rightarrow$ 2. Quantizer rounds pulse heights to $L$ levels $\rightarrow$ 3. Encoder outputs $n$-bit binary words.
PYQ 06 Flat-Top Sampling
The process of creating Pulse Amplitude Modulation (PAM) signals using a sample-and-hold circuit is known as:
  • A. Ideal Sampling
  • B. Natural Sampling
  • C. Flat-Top Sampling ✓
  • D. Delta Sampling
Answer: C — Flat-Top Sampling
Flat-top sampling holds the sample voltage constant across the pulse duration, producing flat-topped PAM pulses.
PYQ 07 Nyquist Rate for 100 MHz Signal
An analog signal has frequency components ranging from $10\text{ MHz}$ to $100\text{ MHz}$. What is the minimum Nyquist sampling rate?
  • A. 100 MHz
  • B. 200 Msamples/sec (200 MHz) ✓
  • C. 110 MHz
  • D. 400 MHz
Answer: B — 200 Msamples/sec
$f_{\max} = 100\text{ MHz}$. Nyquist Rate $f_s \ge 2 \times f_{\max} = 2 \times 100 = \mathbf{200\text{ Msamples/sec}}$.
PYQ 08 Granular Noise in DM
In Delta Modulation, Granular Noise occurs when the input analog signal:
  • A. Is nearly flat/constant relative to the step size ✓
  • B. Has a very steep slope
  • C. Has zero amplitude
  • D. Exceeds power limits
Answer: A
Granular noise occurs when the signal changes very slowly (flat), causing the staircase approximation to hunt/oscillate above and below the signal.
PYQ 09 Parallel vs Serial Wires
To transmit 8 bits of data simultaneously in Parallel transmission, how many physical wires are required?
  • A. 1 wire
  • B. 2 wires
  • C. 8 wires ✓
  • D. 16 wires
Answer: C — 8 wires
Parallel transmission requires $n$ separate wires to send $n$ bits simultaneously in parallel.
PYQ 10 Maximum Quantization Error
If the quantization step size is $\Delta = 4\text{V}$, what is the maximum quantization error?
  • A. $\pm 4\text{V}$
  • B. $\pm 2\text{V}$ ($\Delta / 2$) ✓
  • C. $\pm 1\text{V}$
  • D. $\pm 8\text{V}$
Answer: B — $\pm 2\text{V}$
Maximum Quantization Error = $\pm \frac{\Delta}{2} = \pm \frac{4}{2} = \mathbf{\pm 2\text{V}}$.
