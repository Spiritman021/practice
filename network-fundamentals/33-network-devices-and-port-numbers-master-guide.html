Section 33.2

Collision Domains vs Broadcast Domains

💡
Golden Exam Rule for IBPS SO IT
• A Hub has 1 Collision Domain & 1 Broadcast Domain.
• A Switch divides Collision Domains (1 per port), but maintains 1 Broadcast Domain.
• A Router divides BOTH Collision Domains AND Broadcast Domains (1 of each per interface!).

Section 33.4

High-Yield Port Number Categories & Exam Mnemonics

Email Trio
SMTP (25), POP3 (110), IMAP (143)
Remember: 25 sends email out, 110 pulls email down to 1 machine, 143 syncs email across all devices.
File Transfer Pair
FTP (20/21) vs TFTP (69)
FTP uses TCP Ports 20/21 (reliable); TFTP uses UDP Port 69 (unreliable/fast).
