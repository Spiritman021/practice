Topic 11.5

⚡ IBPS SO IT Officer — Exam Strategy & Numerical Quick Rules

Key shortcuts and rules for solving the 70–80% high-yield numerical questions in the paper:

🔑 1. Candidate Key Shortcut (RHS Rule)

Find attributes that NEVER appear on the RHS of any FD. Those attributes must be present in every Candidate Key. Compute their closure first!

🧩 2. Normal Form Testing Order

Test top-down: Check BCNF (Left side superkey?) → 3NF (Right side prime?) → 2NF (No partial FD?) → 1NF (Atomic).

🔗 3. Lossless Decomposition Condition

For $R \to R_1, R_2$, decomposition is lossless iff $(R_1 \cap R_2) \to R_1$ OR $(R_1 \cap R_2) \to R_2$ (common attribute must be a key in at least one table).

📊 4. Join Tuple Bounds

For Natural Join of $R_1(m \text{ tuples})$ and $R_2(n \text{ tuples})$: Min tuples $= 0$, Max tuples $= m \times n$. If foreign key constraint exists, Max $= \text{referencing table size}$.
